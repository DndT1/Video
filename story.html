The Story Behind Game Playtimes

The NBA 2K Marathon: From Sports Sim to Infinite Grind

When you look at the scatter plot above, one pattern immediately leaps out: the NBA 2K series (highlighted in bright orange) sits far above the vast majority of games in the database. While standard video games with 20-to-40-hour campaigns rarely require more than 80 to 120 hours for full completion, modern NBA 2K titles demand hundreds—and sometimes close to a thousand—hours of commitment.

  • The 950+ Hour Summit (NBA 2K13): Hover near the very top of our scatter plot to find NBA 2K13. At an astronomical 953.6 hours for 100% completion, it is one of the single most time-consuming games ever tracked on HowLongToBeat, rivaling sprawling MMORPGs.
  • The Evolution of “Main Story”: In the franchise’s early era (such as ESPN NBA 2K5 at 8.0 hrs and NBA 2K6 at 1.0 hr), completing a sports title simply meant finishing a few tournament games or a quick season. But starting around NBA 2K11 and 2K12, the introduction of cinematic, narrative-driven MyCareer campaigns pushed main story playthroughs up to 25 to 55+ hours, peaking with NBA 2K22 at 81.7 hours.
  • The 100% Completion Explosion: While recent campaigns have leveled off around 20 to 40 hours, completionist requirements have surged into another stratosphere—NBA 2K24 requires 570.0 hours, NBA 2K22 takes 478.7 hours, and NBA 2K25 demands 430.5 hours. Maxing out badges, reaching 99 overall ratings, collecting MyTeam card sets, and earning seasonal trophies turn an annual sports release into a second full-time job.
  • The Acclaim vs. Time Paradox: Did heavier playtime demands make the games better? The data tells a different story. The peak of critical praise arrived with NBA 2K11 (★ 82) and NBA 2K12 (★ 77)—titles with manageable 100-to-150-hour completion targets. As recent editions ballooned past 400+ hours with heavy Virtual Currency (VC) grinding, review scores drifted steadily into the mid-60s (★ 66 for 2K24 and 2K25).
  • Community Backlog Fatigue: Because each title is replaced after just 12 months, players struggle to keep up. HowLongToBeat tracking shows massive backlogs—NBA 2K17 has over 1,635 players and NBA 2K20 has over 1,098 players who logged the game into their unfinished piles before the next edition arrived.
