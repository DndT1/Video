The Story Behind Game Playtimes

About HowLongToBeat

HowLongToBeat is a community-driven database that crowdsources video game completion times from millions of players worldwide. Instead of relying on a single playtime estimate, the platform breaks play styles down into three core categories:

  • Main Story: Finishing the core objectives to see the end credits without straying into side content.
  • Main + Extras: Completing the main storyline while taking time to explore side quests, optional bosses, and character extras.
  • 100% Completionist: Doing everything possible in the game—unlocking every achievement/trophy, discovering all secrets, and collecting 100% of items.

Our database contains playtime and rating metrics for over 3,100 of the most popular video games, ranging from retro classics to modern releases.

Have Games Really Gotten Longer?

A frequent debate among gamers is whether modern titles demand far more time than older generations. By tracking median completion times across release years (2000–2024), we can see how game lengths have evolved across all three play styles.

Key Takeaways

  1. Main Stories Are Remarkably Stable: For over two decades, the core campaign length of popular games has consistently hovered around 11 to 16 hours.
  2. Completionist Time Has Surged: While the main campaign hasn’t ballooned, the depth of side content and 100% completionist runs has grown significantly with modern open-world game design.
